## 1. Scope & Audit Purpose

Under the [MASTER_PLAN.md](../../docs/work-plan/MASTER_PLAN.md) (Section 5) and [02-thulasi.md](../../docs/work-plan/modules/02-thulasi.md), Task T-02 extends the verified knowledge corpus to at least ten useful reviewed official sources, prioritizing:
- **Product definitions** under the *Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940* and *Drugs Rules, 1945* (Classical ASU, Patent or Proprietary ASU, Phytopharmaceuticals, and Cosmetics).
- **Indian Patent / Traditional Knowledge (TK) provisions** under *The Patents Act, 1970*, *The Biological Diversity Act, 2002*, and administrative examination guidelines from IP India.
- **Ayurveda Aahara regulations and implementation directives** under the *Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)*.

This audit note documents the verification of **12 official instruments** (`REAL-001` through `REAL-012`), 25 provision-level passages (`PASS-001` through `PASS-025`), the formal review log, and the registry of evidence gaps.
